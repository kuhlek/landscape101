About Evergrove

A Gauteng landscaping company built on clear quotes, reliable crews and work that holds up season after season.

Our story

Evergrove started with a two-person crew and a simple rule: quote honestly, finish on time, leave the site clean. Twelve years later we employ over 40 people and care for gardens, estates and commercial properties across Gauteng.

Every project is led by a dedicated project manager, so you always know who is accountable.
