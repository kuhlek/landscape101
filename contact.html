Common questions


Is the site visit really free?

Yes. We visit, measure and discuss your plans at no cost and with no obligation.

How long does a typical project take?

Most residential gardens take two to six weeks. We confirm a schedule in writing with your quote.

Do you offer a guarantee?

All construction carries a five-year workmanship guarantee, and plants are replaced free within 90 days if they fail under our care instructions.

Can you work with my budget?

Yes. We can phase larger projects so you spread the investment over several seasons.

Which areas do you cover?

Pretoria, Centurion, Johannesburg, Midrand and surrounding Gauteng areas.
